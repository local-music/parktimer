Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PARK TIMER is designed to help you manage parking sessions and find your parked car while keeping your parking information on your device.

Information used by the app

Location: With your permission, PARK TIMER uses your device's location to save a parking spot and help guide you back to it.

Camera and photos: If you choose, you can take or select a photo to remember your parking location. Camera-based guidance may use the live camera view.

Parking details: Timers, notes, floor and bay information, saved locations and parking history are stored locally on your device.

Notifications: With permission, PARK TIMER schedules local reminders for your parking timer.

Storage and sharing

PARK TIMER does not require an account. Parking records are stored locally on your device, and we do not operate an account database for them.

We do not sell your personal information or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

When you use Apple Maps or mapping services, Apple may process map requests and related information according to Apple's own privacy terms. If you contact support by email, your message and email address are received so we can respond.

Your choices

You can manage location, camera, photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete saved parking records in the app where supported. Removing the app normally removes its locally stored app data, subject to iOS backups and system behaviour.

Children's privacy

PARK TIMER does not intentionally collect personal information from children through an account or registration system.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.
