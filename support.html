Common questions

How do I save my parking spot?

Start a parking session and save your location. You can also add details such as a floor, bay number, photo or note.

How do I get parking reminders?

Allow notifications when prompted, then set a parking timer. Notifications depend on your device settings.

Why isn't the car finder perfectly accurate?

GPS and compass accuracy vary, especially inside garages and buildings. Directional guidance is approximate; use your saved parking details and nearby signs too.

Does the app require an account?

No account is required.
